The prose styling doing real work, rather than as a specimen.



  
field notes · registries

  
Why the registry is the interesting part

  
Every network has a phone book. The question is who gets to edit it,
  and whether anyone can prove they cheated.

  
The operator is the attack surface

  
When two parties exchange signed messages, the receiver’s real question is not
  is this signature valid but whose key am I checking it against. That answer
  comes from a registry — which makes the registry operator the one party worth compromising.

  
What transparency actually buys

  
An append-only log doesn’t stop the operator from lying. It makes the lie
  provable — which, for institutions, is the property that matters.
